No. It is designed for apprentices, journeymen and foremen. Different features are useful at different experience levels.

No. FitterField is an independent educational and productivity product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NFPA, NCCER or any manufacturer.

Does Pro require a monthly subscription?

Yes. FitterField Pro is a recurring monthly membership. You can cancel through Stripe, and your access remains available according to the subscription status and billing period.

Yes. FitterField is mobile-first and designed for phones, tablets and modern browsers.
